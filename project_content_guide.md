## GENERAL NOTES

### Tone Guidance
Your brand voice reads as: **precise, strategic, controlled but energetic**. Write in a way that sounds like you're briefing someone — direct, no filler words, every sentence earns its place. Think technical documentation meets creative manifesto.

### Content Length
- **Description**: 1-3 sentences. Punchy, visual, immediate.
- **Challenge/Approach/Outcome**: 2-4 sentences each. Enough to tell the story, not so much that it becomes an essay.

### What Makes a Strong Case Study
1. Show the *thinking*, not just the result
2. Reference specific decisions ("I chose coral because...")
3. Connect the visual to the strategy ("The bold palette reflects the podcast's energy because...")
4. Be honest about constraints — they make the work more impressive
